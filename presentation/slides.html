IDEA TITLE: MineGuard AI (KhanRakshak)
SMART INDIAHACKATHON 2026

Core Solution Components

1. PostGIS Spatial & Boundary Geofencing
Maps active pit coordinates against approved DGMS lease boundaries to prevent illegal peripheral extraction.
2. Multi-Factor Explainable AI Risk Scoring
Evaluates open safety violations, overdue actions, compliance deadlines, and night-shift anomalies (0–100 scale).
3. Resilient Offline Mobile Sync
Allows safety inspectors in deep pits with zero cellular signal to record audits locally and sync with idempotency keys.
4. Confidential Worker Grievance Redressal
Empowers miners to submit wage and safety complaints with auto-generated tracking codes (MG-GRV) and strict privacy.
OPERATIONAL FLOW ENGINE
👷 Field Collection: Mobile audits, geo-tagged photos, worker reports.
↓
🔄 FastAPI Gateway: Idempotent validation, deduplication, RBAC.
↓
🧠 AI Risk Engine: scikit-learn scores; PostGIS verifies boundaries.
↓
🔗 SHA-256 Audit: Cryptographic append-only chain verification.
↓
📊 Role Portals: Real-time views for Managers, CIL, Regulators.

Innovation & Uniqueness

Unified Mining Nervous System
First-ever integrated platform uniting DGMS statutory compliance, GIS remote sensing, and worker welfare into a single source of truth.
Cryptographic SHA-256 Audit Trail
Replaces vulnerable paper registers with an append-only cryptographic hash chain in PostgreSQL, guaranteeing zero regulatory tampering.
Explainable AI with Prescriptive Action
Transparent 5-factor scoring model that outputs exact contributing weights and automatically creates time-bound corrective action tickets.

Technology Stack Architecture

API & Backend: FastAPI, Python 3.12+, Uvicorn, RESTful `/api/v1`
Primary Database: PostgreSQL 16 + PostGIS 3.4 Spatial Indexing
Dev Emulation DB: SQLite Dual-Dialect (Zero-config instant local test)
ORM & Migrations: SQLAlchemy 2.0, GeoAlchemy2, Alembic
Validation: Pydantic v2 & Pydantic-Settings
Security & Auth: JWT, Argon2 / Bcrypt, Refresh Token Rotation
AI Risk Scoring: scikit-learn, NumPy Multi-Factor Engine
Computer Vision: Tesseract OCR & Pillow for Statutory Permits
Remote Sensing: Sentinel-1 InSAR & Google Earth Engine Interface
Frontend Portal: KhanRakshak HTML5 / JS Portal & Next.js / Leaflet

End-to-End Operational Pipeline (01 – 08)

01. FIELD DATA COLLECTION
Mobile app captures inspections, geo-tagged hazard photos, and worker grievances.
02. RESILIENT OFFLINE SYNC
Idempotency keys prevent duplicate audits from deep pits lacking cellular signal.
03. POSTGIS SPATIAL CHECKS
Boundary queries automatically detect illegal mining beyond statutory leases.
04. AI RISK & ANOMALY SCORING
scikit-learn engine computes 0-100 risk score based on 5 weighted dimensions.
05. CRYPTOGRAPHIC AUDIT LOG
Every transaction linked to previous SHA-256 hash for immutable defensibility.
06. AUTOMATED ESCALATION
Configurable engine escalates overdue actions to DGMS Regional Inspectors.
07. ROLE-BASED DASHBOARDS
Custom interfaces tailored for Mine Managers, Corporate CIL, and Regulators.
08. CORRECTIVE ACTION CLOSURE
Enforces formal verification before any serious safety violation is resolved.

Balancing Challenges with Innovative Solutions

⚠️ Connectivity Gaps in Deep Pits
Zero cellular signal deep in opencast quarries and underground coal seams.
✅ Offline Sync with Idempotency
Local SQLite collection synced safely when connectivity resumes (POST /sync).
⚠️ Worker Fear of Retaliation
Workers avoid reporting safety or wage issues due to contractor pressure.
✅ Confidential RBAC Isolation
Encrypted complaints; contractors are restricted from worker-private data.
⚠️ Complex Statutory Red Tape
Dozens of DGMS/CPCB mandates tracked manually across physical books.
✅ Automated Rule Calendar
Pre-configured statutory templates with automatic escalation alerts.
⚠️ Data Tampering by Operators
Risk of colliery managers retroactively altering inspection and hazard records.
✅ Cryptographic SHA-256 Chain
Append-only parent hash linking with instant tamper detection (/audit/verify).
